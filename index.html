Who and how POWER is used can affect someone's sense of BELONGING.



MASKING, or hiding parts of who we are, becomes a defense mechanism to avoid rejection, judgement, or harm in social settings.



This research kit aims to look at the correlation of Power and Belonging through the lived experiences of queer people.



Since masking is a universal experience, if you don't identify as queer, we invite you to relate and understand queer experience with a little reflection at the end.
